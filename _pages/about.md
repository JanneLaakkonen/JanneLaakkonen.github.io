I am a doctoral researcher at the School of Computing, University of Eastern Finland, and a member of the [Finnish Doctoral Program Network in Artificial Intelligence (AI-DOC)](https://fcai.fi/ai-doc/about), hosted by the Finnish Center for Artificial Intelligence (FCAI).

My research focuses on generalization under distribution shift: how models trained on a limited set of conditions remain reliable under conditions they have never encountered. I study this through meta-learning, continual learning, and test-time adaptation, with a particular focus on audio deepfake detection, where new synthesis methods can emerge faster than training data can cover them. I also work on the information geometry underlying these problems.
